01 / COLOR

Let the graph own the color.

Neutral graphite surfaces; a soft blue for focus. Keep lane colors distinct and match refs to their lanes. Use green and red for additions and deletions, with explicit + / − labels.

02 / TYPOGRAPHY

Three levels, clear priorities.

13px commit subjects, 11px supporting metadata, and a 21px inspector title. Use sans serif for reading; reserve monospace for hashes, paths, and refs. Let spacing do more than font weight.

03 / INSPECTOR

A reading panel, not a form.

Group author and time. Bring changed files forward, move long identifiers into Metadata, and replace repeated dividing lines with space. A 350px panel gives titles and paths more room.

04 / DEPTH & DENSITY

Subtle layers. Precise selection.

Separate the frame, graph, and inspector with three surface tones. Keep rows flat, with one tinted selection and a thin leading marker. Compare 42px comfortable rows with 34px compact rows above.
